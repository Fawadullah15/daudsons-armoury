STATUTORY & CORPORATE GOVERNANCE

Licensing Compliance, Privacy Policy & Terms

1. Firearms Licensing & Compliance

All firearm purchases in Pakistan require a valid KP Arms License & Demand Note (for residents of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa) or a valid All-Pakistan Arms License & Demand Note (for residents of other provinces). Direct purchase is conducted at our Factory Outlet (Industrial Estate, Kohat Road, Peshawar) or via authorized dealers. Zero mail/courier delivery is permitted for firearms or licensed parts.

2. Warranty & Maintenance Policy

DSA firearms carry a 1-year warranty with parts covering manufacturing defects only (excluding misuse or negligence), valid solely at DSA factory premises in Peshawar. Transportation is the responsibility of the customer or dealer. Spare parts are guaranteed for 5 years on payment.

3. Privacy & Cookie Governance

Enquiries submitted via our contact or dealership forms are encrypted and used exclusively by DAUDSONS ARMOURY (PVT.) LTD. to process institutional RFQs, customer support, or dealer verification.
